## Experience rules
1. The page is a fixed cinematic stage; scroll is the camera.
2. Each chapter owns a unique physical language.
3. A&O is absent during brand chapters unless narrative requires a trace.
4. Business CTAs are in-world portal objects, not rectangular buttons.
5. Transitions must be causal: material leaves one chapter and seeds the next.
6. Copy animates as editorial composition synchronized with scene phases.
7. One soundtrack, one persistent sound toggle; no playlist UI.
8. A&O ST replaces Master Money as chapter 06 and remains a prepared future portal.
9. Marel/mini-game is not active in V7; only its integration contract is reserved.
10. Mobile uses a reduced visual density and preserves narrative order.
